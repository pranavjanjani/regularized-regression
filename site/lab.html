3 · Choosing the penalty honestly

Three curves. Training error only worsens as α grows, so it is not evidence. Choose on the cross-validated curve; report the held-out one.

Read the curve before you believe the theory

With all 150 training trips and 8 well-behaved features, the CV curve is flat and its minimum sits at the smallest α on the grid. Regularization buys nothing here, and the honest conclusion is that this problem does not need it. Drag the sample size down to 25 or 15 trips and a proper U appears: regularization answers a shortage of data relative to parameters.
